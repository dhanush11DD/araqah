About us

Deep-rooted trust, modern service

Araqah Recruitment Office is an officially licensed Saudi recruitment office based in Hittin, Riyadh. We specialise in recruiting trained domestic workers for families across the Kingdom.

We believe a family's comfort starts with choosing the right person. That is why we work with accredited partner agencies in 10 countries to bring you qualified workers through clear, regulated procedures at competitive prices.

Our name, Araqah, means heritage and deep roots. It reflects how we work: honest dealings, commitments kept, and a team that stays with you after the contract is signed.
